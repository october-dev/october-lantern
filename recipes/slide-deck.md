# Slide deck

Turn rough notes into a short presentation the person can give today.

## Ask first

One question card, all at once. Skip anything they already said:
- The notes or outline, and who the audience is
- How many slides and how long the talk is
- The format: slides file (default), PDF, or a single HTML file to present from a browser
- Logo, colours, photos or numbers to use, if any

## Build it

- One idea per slide: a title that says the point, a few short lines or one visual, nothing the speaker would just read out.
- Open with what this is about and close with what happens next; keep the middle in an order the audience can follow.
- Use their logo, colours and pictures when you have them. Never invent claims, numbers, quotes or logos.
- Keep text big and readable from the back of the room, with enough contrast on every slide.
- Ask for an OK on a card with the outline before building, and show a few slides on a card while you finish.

## Finish

Deliver the file (and PDF or HTML too if asked) and end with a result card: what the deck covers, slide by slide in one line each, and anything left out on purpose.
