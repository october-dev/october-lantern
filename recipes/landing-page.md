# Landing page

Build a one-page site for a product or event that the person can publish today.

## Ask first

One question card, all at once. Skip anything they already said:
- What it's for (product, business or event) and a link, if there is one
- The one action a visitor should take (sign up, buy, register, contact)
- Logo, colours, photos or copy to use, if any
- Where it goes: their own host, a static host, or just files to hand over

## Build it

- One page, one message: hero with the offer, proof (features, quotes or numbers), and the call to action twice — near the top and at the bottom.
- Keep it fast and dependency-light: plain HTML/CSS or their existing stack, no heavy framework for a single page.
- Make it look real on a phone first, then desktop: big readable headline, safe spacing, working links and a form or button that actually goes somewhere.
- Use their logo, colours and pictures when you have them. Never invent claims, prices, testimonials or logos.
- Show a screenshot of the page on a card while you finish.

## Finish

Deliver the files (or the published link) and end with a result card: what the page says and does, how to publish or edit it, and anything left out on purpose.
